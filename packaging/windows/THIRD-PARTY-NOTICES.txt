PyInstaller bootloader: GPL with the bootloader distribution exception.

PyAudioWPatch / PortAudio: MIT and PortAudio license, respectively.
pycaw, pyvda, screen_brightness_control, comtypes, WMI and audioop-lts:

GTCRN denoising model: MIT; upstream license included.
Rust replay helper: Jarvis MIT code, Rust standard library (MIT/Apache-2.0
and bundled notices), serde_json/serde_core/itoa/libc/memchr (MIT alternative)
and zmij (MIT). See licenses/replay-store/ for upstream license texts and
Rust standard-library copyright notices. Its exact Cargo versions are in
native/replay-store/Cargo.lock in the source distribution.

The manifest records the exact dependency versions and SHA-256 hashes of
bundled files. licenses/ also contains notices for supporting Python packages
collected from their installed distribution metadata. Upstream files retain
their own copyright holders and license terms.

FFmpeg 8.1 + x264 are separate subprocess executables under GPLv3-or-later.
FFmpeg is built without network protocols or nonfree components, with x264,
NVIDIA codec headers, AMD AMF headers, Intel libvpl and LLVM-MinGW runtimes.
Their notices and complete corresponding source/used headers/build recipe
are supplied in FFmpeg-Corresponding-Source.tar.xz and licenses/.
The Intel dispatcher includes one compatibility patch to restrict an old
MSVC fallback to actual old MSVC compilers. The unused gfxcapture filter is
disabled; DDA capture remains enabled. No FFmpeg source patch is applied.
